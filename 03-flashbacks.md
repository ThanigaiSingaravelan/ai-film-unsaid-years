# Flashbacks

College, campus, classroom, and the mirror sequence that triggers them.

---

## 3A. Classroom (30s, six shots)

Uses the **THANE college** and **NEETHAL college** blocks.

**Setting:** A plain college classroom, warm daylight through tall windows on one side, rows of simple wooden desks, a plain green board at the front. A teacher stands at the board, back mostly to class, writing and speaking. Around twenty students, most in soft focus.

**Lighting:** Warm afternoon light, dust in the air, low contrast, lifted blacks, soft highlights, fine grain, shallow depth of field. Warmer and softer than present day.

**Shot 1 (0–5s) The room**
Wide from the back corner. The teacher writes at the board, speaking. Students at desks, heads down, some writing. Warm light streaming across the rows. Thane sits mid-room, third row. She sits at a back bench, further from camera, soft in the frame.

**Shot 2 (5–10s) The turn**
Medium close-up on Thane at his desk, pen in hand, apparently listening. Then he turns his head and looks back over his shoulder, past the rows behind him. He holds the look a beat too long.

**Shot 3 (10–15s) What he sees**
His point of view, over his shoulder: the back benches in shallow focus. She sits with her chin on her hand, half attending, looking toward the board. She does not notice him. Sunlight across the desk in front of her. Soft, slightly hazy.

**Shot 4 (15–20s) Caught**
Back on Thane. Her head begins to turn toward him. He snaps forward instantly, eyes down to his notebook, pen moving. A small tightness in his jaw. He does not look back again.

**Shot 5 (20–25s) After class**
Wide. The classroom is looser now, students standing, bags lifting, the teacher gone. She stands at the back with two other girls, talking and laughing, animated, her back half to camera. Thane stands near the front with one male friend, who is talking to him.

**Shot 6 (25–30s) Still watching**
Medium close-up on Thane. His friend beside him is mid-conversation, gesturing, clearly speaking to him. Thane nods once without hearing a word. His eyes are fixed past his friend's shoulder. Focus racks briefly to the soft blur of her laughing in the background, then back to his face. He smiles faintly at something that is not the joke his friend is telling.

## 3B. Campus path (30s, six shots)

Uses the **NEETHAL** and **LIGHTING memory campus** blocks.

**Shot 1 (0–5s)** Over-the-shoulder from behind Thane, his shoulder and the edge of his glasses soft in the left foreground. She stands a few feet away on a tree-lined campus path, turned toward him, looking back over her shoulder. Students pass far behind in heavy bokeh. She holds his gaze, then glances down.

**Shot 2 (5–10s)** Reverse: over her shoulder onto Thane. He stands still, expression unchanged, sunlight catching one lens. Leaves move behind him.

**Shot 3 (10–15s)** Medium two-shot, side angle. They walk slowly together along the path, not talking, a small gap between them. Dappled light moves across both.

**Shot 4 (15–20s)** Close on her. She looks off toward something out of frame, then back. A slight tilt of the head. Warm light on one cheek.

**Shot 5 (20–25s)** Wide. The two of them small in a sunlit campus courtyard, seated on a low stone bench, a gap of empty bench between them. Trees frame the top of the shot.

**Shot 6 (25–30s)** Back to over-the-shoulder on her from behind Thane. She turns to face forward and walks away down the path, growing smaller, staying in focus as the foreground shoulder blurs. Hold on the empty path. Fade out.

---

## 3C. Campus path, continued (30s, six shots)

**Shot 7 (0–5s) He follows**
Wide, low angle from ground level on the path. Thane steps into frame from the near side and walks after her, unhurried. She is far ahead, small, still walking. Dappled light crosses him as he passes under the trees. He does not call out.

**Shot 8 (5–10s) She stops**
Medium on her from the front, walking toward camera. She slows, then stops. She looks back over her shoulder, down the path behind her. Hair moves in the light breeze. She waits.

**Shot 9 (10–15s) Catching up**
Medium two-shot from the side. He reaches her. They stand a step apart, both facing the same direction down the path rather than at each other. Neither speaks. Leaves drift past between them and the camera.

**Shot 10 (15–20s) The gap**
Close on their hands at their sides, framed waist-down, walking. His hand and hers swing near each other, close but never touching. Warm light on their sleeves. Shallow focus.

**Shot 11 (20–25s) Golden hour**
Wide. The light has warmed and lowered, long shadows stretching across the path, sun flaring softly through branches. The two of them walk away from camera, side by side, small in a big frame.

**Shot 12 (25–30s) Hold**
Very wide, static. They walk on until they are small figures at the end of the path. The trees arch overhead. The frame holds after they have nearly gone. Light shifts gently through the leaves. Fade out.

**Note:** shot 10 is the emotional centre of the sequence. Hands nearly touching says what the voiceover says about six years of near-misses, without a word. It is also the shot most likely to produce finger artefacts, so it took the most attempts.

## 3D. The mirror (30s, six shots)

The present-day frame that triggers the flashbacks. Lightning motivates each cut.

Uses the **THANE present day** block, plus:

> **Face lock:** the expression stays exactly the same throughout. Calm, still, quietly serious, lips closed, eyes steady. Do not change the expression. No smiling, no frowning, no crying, no eyebrow movement. Only blinking and small head movement.

**Lighting:** Dim cool-blue ambient night light, one warm lamp glow from camera left, rain on the window. Lightning flashes are soft and rolling, a brief bloom of cool white light that swells and fades, not a harsh strobe.

**Shot 1 (0–5s) The mirror**
He rises and crosses to a plain rectangular mirror on the bedroom wall. He stops and looks at his reflection, expression unchanged. Medium shot from behind his shoulder. Rain-light patterns move across the wall. A soft lightning bloom washes the room and fades.

**Shot 2 (5–10s) First flash**
Close on his reflection, expression held. A lightning bloom swells and dissolves through white into daylight: a wide outdoor college walkway, warm sunlight, students passing in soft focus. He sits on a stone bench beside a young woman seen from behind, face turned away. Warm, slightly overexposed, handheld. The light blooms again and returns to his face in the mirror, expression identical.

**Shot 3 (10–15s) Second flash**
Flash. A small canteen, warm tungsten light, two glasses of tea on a laminate table. The same young woman opposite him, back to camera, out of focus. Shallow focus, warm grade. Flash returns to the mirror. His face is unchanged.

**Shot 4 (15–20s) Third flash**
Flash. An indoor corridor, cool daylight from tall windows. He stands still at one end. Far down the corridor, two figures in soft silhouette, one holding out a flower. Wide frame, he is small in it. Flash back to the mirror. His face is unchanged.

**Shot 5 (20–25s) Fourth flash**
Flash. A café interior, muted daylight. A folded paper letter slides across a table between two hands. Close on the letter, then a hand pulling back. Cold, still framing. Flash back to the mirror. Rain-light runs down over his reflection.

**Shot 6 (25–30s) Return**
Close on his reflection. The flashes stop. Only rain-light and the warm lamp remain. He holds his own gaze in the glass, same steady expression. A long beat. He turns away from the mirror. Camera holds on the empty glass, rain-light moving across it. Fade to black.

**Note:** mirrors are hard. Asking for the back of a head and its reflection in the same frame often produces two different people. Framing so that only the reflection is visible solves it.

## Negative prompt, all flashback sequences

```
logos, brand names, watermarks, signage, readable text on boards or in notebooks,
screens, phones, celebrity likeness, recognisable actor, real person, film still,
letterbox bars, changing face between shots, warped glasses, changing hair length,
clothing changing between shots, extra fingers, distorted hands, hands touching,
harsh strobing, rapid flicker, harsh contrast, fast camera moves, lens flares,
changing facial expression, smiling, frowning, crying, exaggerated emotion
```
