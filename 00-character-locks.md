# Character and lighting locks

These blocks are pasted into every prompt unchanged. Nothing in them is reworded between shots.

---

## THANE — present day

A 24-year-old South Asian man with short black hair, thin dark-rimmed glasses, light stubble, wearing a plain cream button-up shirt. Original character, generic appearance, not based on any real or known person. Identical face, glasses and clothing in every shot.

## THANE — college years

A 19-year-old South Asian man, short black hair, thin dark-rimmed glasses, clean-shaven, wearing a plain light blue shirt. Original character, generic appearance, not based on any real or known person. Identical face, glasses and clothing in every shot.

## THANE — the double (internal confrontation)

Present Thane: plain cream button-up shirt, lit cool blue from the window side.
Past Thane: plain dark grey t-shirt, no glasses, lit warm from the lamp side.
Identical faces on both figures. Wardrobe and lighting carry the distinction.

## THANE — waking

Plain white vest, no glasses, lying in bed. Glasses folded on the bedside table.

---

## NEETHAL

A 24-year-old South Asian woman with long dark wavy hair worn loose over one shoulder, a small dark bindi, minimal makeup, wearing a soft cream-beige cotton kurta with a draped dupatta. Warm medium-brown skin, calm oval face, dark eyes. Original character, generic appearance, not based on any real or known person. Identical face, hair and clothing in every shot.

## NEETHAL — college years

An 18-year-old South Asian woman with long dark wavy hair worn loose over one shoulder, a small dark bindi, wearing a plain cream kurta. Original character, generic appearance, not based on any real or known person.

---

## LIGHTING — present day, UK

16:9, 4K. Dim cool-blue ambient light, one warm lamp glow from camera left, soft cool white light from a source in front of him. Low-saturation grade, teal shadows, soft blacks, fine grain, shallow depth of field. Natural human motion.

## LIGHTING — memory, campus

16:9, 4K. Warm afternoon sunlight through trees, dappled light, soft haze. Warm low-contrast grade, lifted blacks, fine grain, shallow depth of field, background falling into soft bokeh.

## LIGHTING — Neethal's room, India

16:9, 4K. Dim warm ambient light, one warm bedside lamp glow from camera right, soft cool white light from a source in front of her. Low-saturation grade, soft blacks, fine grain, shallow depth of field.

## LIGHTING — imagined future

16:9, 4K. Bright, warm, open light. Higher key and warmer than the rest of the film. Soft golden tones, gentle highlights, low contrast, lifted blacks, fine grain, shallow depth of field. Slightly dreamlike, slightly overexposed at the edges.

---

## Notes

The age gap between the college and present-day blocks is the only thing that changes about Thane. Keeping the wardrobe fixed within each era does more for continuity than any other single choice.

Lighting written as a system and repeated verbatim produces a far more consistent grade than describing the light fresh in each shot.
