# Inserts

Short cutaways. Each generated as a single pass with minimal motion, because the subjects here are the ones models handle worst.

---

## 4A. Eyes (5s)

Extreme close-up on his eyes, framed from mid-forehead to the bridge of the nose. Shot through his glasses, the lenses catching a soft rectangular reflection of the laptop screen, cool white against dark brown irises. Very shallow depth of field: eyelashes and iris texture sharp, the frame of the glasses falling soft.

Cool blue ambient fill, screen glow as the only key light, a faint warm rim from camera left along the temple.

**Motion over the full 5s:** he is looking slightly off-axis, down and away. Around 1.5s his gaze lifts and settles directly into the lens. A single slow blink at roughly 3s. The eyes stay open after it, steady, faintly glassy, a thin film of moisture catching the screen light without a tear falling. The screen reflection in the lenses shifts subtly in brightness throughout. Camera holds nearly static with only a micro-drift closer.

Natural anatomy, correct human eye structure, symmetrical gaze, both pupils tracking together.

**Negative:** tear rolling down the face, red or bloodshot eyes, distorted or asymmetric irises, extra reflections, warped glasses frame, rapid blinking, eye movement out of sync between left and right, text or UI in frame, fast camera moves.

**Variant:** for the version after the confession lands, change the final motion line to *the moisture builds until the lower lid cannot hold it, and one tear breaks free at around 4s*, and remove the tear from the negatives.

## 4B. Hands (5s)

Close-up on his hands resting on the wooden desk in front of the laptop, framed from mid-forearm down. Camera slightly above, looking down at a shallow angle. Very shallow depth of field, knuckles and fingertips sharp, the laptop edge and desk surface falling soft behind.

Cool blue ambient fill, cool white laptop-screen glow raking across the tops of the hands from the front, one warm practical lamp accent from camera left catching the edge of the wrist. Skin texture visible, faint sheen on the knuckles.

**Motion over the full 5s:** the fingers of one hand tap the desk once, twice, then stop. The hands come together, fingers interlacing slowly, gripping until the knuckles pale slightly. A held beat. Then they release, and one thumb rubs across the opposite palm, back and forth, twice. The hands settle flat on the desk, still, but the fingertips press down. Screen light shifts subtly in brightness throughout. Camera nearly static with a micro-drift closer.

Anatomically correct human hands, exactly two hands, exactly five fingers on each, natural joint articulation, natural proportions.

**Negative:** extra fingers, missing fingers, fused or merged fingers, extra hands, distorted knuckles, unnatural joint bending, rubbery or melting skin, blurred fingertips, jewellery, rings, watch, face in frame, text or UI in frame, fast camera moves, exaggerated shaking.

**Note:** interlaced fingers is the hardest pose for a model to solve. A version where the hands stay separated, one flat on the desk and the other tapping then gripping the desk edge, produced far fewer artefacts. Fewer contact points, fewer failures.

## 4C. Breath into heartbeat (10s)

One continuous move. The heart is specified as a stylised light form rather than an anatomical organ, which is both safer for content filters and better suited to the film.

**0–4s, breathing.** Close-up on his chest and upper torso, framed from collarbone to mid-ribs, face out of frame above. The cream shirt catches cool white laptop-screen glow from the front and a warm practical accent from camera left. Shallow depth of field. His chest rises and falls, first a shallow uneven breath, then a held pause where the movement almost stops, then a slow deliberate exhale that drops the shoulders. Fabric shifts subtly with each breath. Camera nearly static.

**4–7s, the push.** The camera begins a slow continuous dolly straight in toward the centre of his chest. As it moves, the frame darkens at the edges, the fabric texture softens, and the surrounding room falls away into black. The screen glow narrows to a single soft pool of light at the centre of frame. Movement stays smooth and unbroken, no cuts.

**7–10s, the heart.** The light at the centre resolves into a stylised heart form rendered as soft warm luminous energy, a glowing silhouette of gentle light and haze suspended in darkness, no anatomical detail, no tissue, no blood, no veins. It pulses in a steady two-beat rhythm, each pulse sending a faint ring of light outward that fades. The glow shifts from cool white to warm amber across the final beats, brightening slightly with each pulse. Hold on the pulsing light. Fade to black.

**Negative:** anatomical organ, realistic heart, blood, veins, tissue, flesh, X-ray, medical imagery, open chest, wound, bare skin, text or UI in frame, face in frame, fast or jerky camera moves, harsh strobing.

**Note:** the morph at 7s was the unreliable part. Models tend to cut rather than transform. Generating it as two 5s pieces, the breathing push-in and the pulsing light alone, then dissolving between them in edit, was more dependable than fighting for it in one pass.
