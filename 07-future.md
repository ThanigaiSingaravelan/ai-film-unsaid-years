# The future

The life he is imagining. Graded warmer and brighter than anything else in the film, so it reads as hope rather than memory.

In every shot they are together but not touching, except the last. The whole film has been six years of nearly-touching hands, so the payoff lands hardest if the gap holds until the final frame.

## 7A. Mall, cinema, beach (30s, six shots)

**Continuity, pasted identically into every shot:**

> **THANE:** A 24-year-old South Asian man, short black hair, thin dark-rimmed glasses, light stubble, wearing a plain cream button-up shirt. Original character, generic appearance, not based on any real or known person. Identical face, glasses and clothing in every shot.
>
> **NEETHAL:** A 24-year-old South Asian woman with long dark wavy hair worn loose over one shoulder, a small dark bindi, wearing a soft cream-beige cotton kurta. Original character, generic appearance, not based on any real or known person. Identical face, hair and clothing in every shot.
>
> 16:9, 4K. Bright, warm, open light. Higher key and warmer than the rest of the film. Soft golden tones, gentle highlights, low contrast, lifted blacks, fine grain, shallow depth of field, backgrounds falling into soft bokeh. Imagined-future look, slightly dreamlike, slightly overexposed at the edges. Natural human motion. Both smiling easily throughout.

**Shot 1 (0–5s) Mall, walking**
Medium tracking shot from the front, moving backwards ahead of them. They walk side by side through a bright open shopping concourse, plain unbranded storefronts heavily out of focus behind. Warm overhead light. She says something, he laughs. Shoppers pass in soft blur.

**Shot 2 (5–10s) Mall, a pause**
Medium two-shot, static. They stop in front of a shop window. She points at something off-frame. He leans in slightly to look where she is pointing, then looks at her instead. Reflected light on the glass beside them.

**Shot 3 (10–15s) Cinema**
Two-shot in a dark cinema auditorium, lit only by the flicker of the screen off-frame. They sit beside each other in plain seats, faces washed in soft shifting light. She laughs at something on screen. He is watching the screen, then glances sideways at her, then back. Shallow focus, warm flicker.

**Shot 4 (15–20s) Beach, wide**
Wide shot, golden hour. A long open beach, sea to one side, sun low. The two of them walking away from camera along the wet sand, small in a big frame, backlit and rim-lit by the sun. Long shadows stretching behind them. Gentle waves.

**Shot 5 (20–25s) Beach, close**
Medium two-shot from the side, walking. Warm low sun across both faces, hair moving in the sea breeze. She turns her head toward him mid-sentence. He is already looking at her. Neither looks away.

**Shot 6 (25–30s) The hands**
Close on their hands at their sides as they walk, framed waist-down, wet sand and low sun behind. The hands swing near each other, the same near-miss as before. Then, without hesitation, hers finds his and they hold. Cut wide: the two of them continuing down the beach, hand in hand, into the light. Hold. Fade out.

## Note

Shot 6 is the payoff of the entire film and the riskiest generation in it. Two hands joining is where finger artefacts are most likely, and it took more attempts than any other shot.

The fallback, if it will not come clean, is cutting to the wide a beat earlier so the audience sees them already holding hands rather than watching it happen. Slightly less powerful, far more reliable.
