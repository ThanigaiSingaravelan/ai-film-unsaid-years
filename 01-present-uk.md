# Present day, UK

Thane alone in his room. The opening and the build to the call.

---

## 1A. The message (10s, four shots)

**Continuity:** The man sits in his dimly lit bedroom at night, wearing his cream shirt, lit by cool blue ambient light with a warm practical lamp glow on one side. Quiet tone, realistic motion, soft film grain. 16:9, 4K.

**Shot 1 (0–2.5s)** Medium shot, slightly low angle. He sits back in his chair, glances at the phone lying face-down on the desk, hesitates for a beat, then reaches over and picks it up. The screen lights up as he raises it, casting a cool white glow onto his face in the dim room.

**Shot 2 (2.5–5s)** Over-the-shoulder close-up on the phone screen, shallow depth of field, his shoulder soft in the foreground. A simple chat app with a dark interface. His thumbs type a short message and hit send. The message bubble slides up into the conversation.

**Shot 3 (5–7.5s)** Back to his face, medium close-up. He lowers the phone slightly, exhales, eyes drifting off. A beat. The phone buzzes softly in his hand, the screen glow flickers brighter against his glasses. His eyes snap back down.

**Shot 4 (7.5–10s)** Over-the-shoulder on the screen: an incoming reply bubble appears, a typing indicator resolving into the message. Cut to a tight close-up of his face: a slow, involuntary smile spreading, half relief, half nostalgia. Hold on the smile in the phone-glow. Cut to black.

**Note:** on-screen text failed more often than it worked. The chat interface was eventually composited in post rather than generated.

## 1B. Opening the laptop (30s, six shots)

Uses the **THANE present day** and **LIGHTING present day UK** blocks.

**Shot 1 (0–5s) The decision**
Medium shot, slightly low angle. He places his phone down on the desk, screen still glowing. His hand moves to a closed laptop and rests on the lid for a moment without opening it. He exhales and gives a small nod to himself. Then he lifts the lid. The screen wakes and casts cool white light across his face with soft bloom. He blinks once at the brightness.

**Shot 2 (5–10s) The hesitation**
Over-the-shoulder, shallow depth of field, shoulder soft in foreground. A minimal dark-interface chat application fills the laptop screen. He scrolls a short contact list, stops on a name, and opens the conversation. The cursor drifts toward a small circular video-call icon at the top right, hovers, then moves away. Cut to his face, medium close-up: focused, unblinking.

**Shot 3 (10–15s) Gathering courage**
Tight close-up, gentle handheld drift. He rubs the back of his neck, then rests his hand on the desk. He glances off-frame toward the window, then back to the screen. His fingers tap the desk edge twice. He sits up straighter, adjusts his glasses, and squares his shoulders.

**Shot 4 (15–20s) The click**
Over-the-shoulder on the laptop. The cursor returns to the video-call icon. A held beat. Then the click. The interface changes to an outgoing call screen with a small self-view rectangle in the lower-right corner showing only his own face. The far side of the call is empty. Screen light pulses gently.

**Shot 5 (20–25s) Waiting**
Medium close-up, lit by the call-screen glow. Subtle rhythmic light shifts suggest a ringing tone. He watches the screen, eyes flicking down and back up. His hand lifts slightly toward the trackpad, then settles back on the desk. He shifts in his chair.

**Shot 6 (25–30s) Connection**
Over-the-shoulder: the status changes. The self-view panel remains in the corner. Snap cut to a tight close-up as the screen light shifts warmer and brighter across his face. His eyebrows lift and a small, warm smile appears. Hold on the smile in the changing glow. Cut to black.

**Note:** the self-view rectangle is the trick that lets a video-call screen read as a call while only one face is ever on it. It keeps the other character off screen until the reveal.

## 1C. Waking (30s, six shots)

Uses the **THANE waking** block.

**Lighting:** Small dim bedroom, very early morning. Cool blue pre-dawn light through a rain-streaked window, one small warm lamp still on from the night before.

**Shot 1 (0–5s)** Tight close-up on his face on the pillow, eyes closed, seen from slightly above. His eyelids move. Then his eyes open, fast, fully awake in one beat. He does not move otherwise. Breathing quick.

**Shot 2 (5–10s)** Close-up. His eyes move around the room without his head turning. Slowly registering. The quickness in his breath settles. He blinks hard once and swallows.

**Shot 3 (10–15s)** Medium shot from the side. He pushes himself upright and sits on the edge of the bed, feet on the floor, shoulders forward, hands hanging between his knees. He rubs both palms down his face and holds them there for a beat.

**Shot 4 (15–20s)** Close on the bedside table: folded glasses, a phone face-down beside them. His hand enters frame, picks up the glasses, and puts them on. Cut to his face as the room comes into focus for him.

**Shot 5 (20–25s)** Medium close-up. He turns the phone over. Screen light on his face. No readable interface. He stares at it.

**Shot 6 (25–30s)** He looks up toward the window, where the sky is beginning to lighten. A long still beat. Then he looks back down at the phone and his thumb moves, decided. Camera pushes in gently. Hold. Cut to black.

## 1D. The double (30s, six shots)

Uses the **THANE double** block.

**Lighting:** Small dim bedroom at night, rain on the window, one warm lamp at camera left, cool blue ambient fill.

**Shot 1 (0–5s)** Medium on Present Thane standing in the middle of the room. Behind him, out of focus, a second figure sits on the edge of the bed, same build, dark t-shirt, head down. Focus racks from Present Thane to the seated figure, revealing the same face.

**Shot 2 (5–10s)** Over-the-shoulder from behind Past Thane onto Present Thane. Present Thane stands rigid, jaw set, speaking without sound, controlled and clipped. His hand cuts once through the air.

**Shot 3 (10–15s)** Reverse: over Present Thane's shoulder onto Past Thane, still seated, looking up. He shakes his head slowly, then looks away toward the window. Unbothered, almost tired.

**Shot 4 (15–20s)** Two-shot, side angle, both in frame. Present Thane steps toward the bed. Past Thane rises to meet him. They stand close, chest to chest, neither touching. The same height, the same face, one cool-lit and one warm-lit.

**Shot 5 (20–25s)** Tight two-shot, faces close, profile to profile. Both still. Present Thane's breathing visible in his shoulders. A long beat.

**Shot 6 (25–30s)** Wide, static. Present Thane stands alone in the centre of the room. The bed is empty. He lowers his head, then lifts it. Camera holds. Fade to black.

**Note:** shots 2 and 3 are over-the-shoulder, so only one face is ever in focus. Those generated cleanly. The static frames (1, 4, 5, 6) were the ones that needed compositing.
